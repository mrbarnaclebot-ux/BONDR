Terms

Short on purpose. Last updated 19 Oct 2026.

01What BONDR is

BONDR is a hobby project: a site that launches and trades Solana memecoins through pump.fun's public programs. It is software, not a broker, exchange or adviser. It is run by one person.

02Your wallet, your responsibility

You connect with Phantom and sign every transaction yourself. BONDR never holds your keys. Transactions on Solana are final; nobody, including BONDR, can reverse them.

03Fees

Trading fees are set by pump: 2.45% on the bonding curve (0.95% protocol, 1.5% creator) and 1.75% on PumpSwap after bond. BONDR adds no platform fee. Creator fees on BONDR coins follow the mechanic shown on each coin's public ledger: before bond they force the bond and burn supply, with 10% to the BONDR treasury; after bond the deployer gets 80% and the treasury 20%.

04Launching

When you launch, your dev buy is sold or burned in the same transaction, as you choose. This cannot be undone. Names, symbols and images must not be illegal or impersonate someone. BONDR may hide a coin from its lists or block a wallet from launching; it cannot remove a coin from Solana.

05Risk

Memecoins are speculative and can go to zero in minutes. Only use money you can lose. Nothing on BONDR is financial advice.

06Your data

With your consent BONDR stores your wallet address, your consent time, your launches and trades through BONDR, and error reports. Ask the owner to delete the off-chain records at any time; on-chain data is public and permanent.

07No warranty

BONDR is provided as is. Pump can change its programs without notice, so features may pause or break, and launches may stop while BONDR's own token migrates.

08Changes and contact

These terms may change; the date above shows when. Continuing to use BONDR after a change means you accept it. Questions go to the owner through the BONDR X account.
