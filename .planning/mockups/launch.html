What happens to your dev buy required

BONDR only co-signs your launch if this exit is inside the transaction you sign.

Where the 1.5% creator fee goes
  • Before bond: 90% builds a burn budget, 10% to the BONDR treasury.
  • At 105% of the cost to finish the curve: the budget buys the rest and burns it.
  • After bond: 80% to you, 20% to the treasury, permanently.

No bond within 24 hours? That window's budget buys and burns BONDR instead.
