| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-07 | Elevation of privilege | send.ts sendTx | mitigate | checkMessage allowlist runs before signing; any other top-level program refuses to sign (unit-tested) |
| T-01-08 | Tampering | net guard | mitigate | All sends go through Ctx.conn from connect(); devnet genesis enforced (plan 01-01) |

| T-01-10 | Repudiation | results rows | accept | Every row carries the full signature, so the chain is the audit trail; no extra logging |
| T-01-10 | Denial of service | Helius free tier | mitigate | paceMs 1100 on devnet; Connection 429 retry |
