## Phase Goal

**As a** dev, **I want to** launch a coin in one Phantom signature, **so that** buyers see I hold zero launch supply.

<objective>
Stand up apps/worker: env and mainnet guard, the private bearer-token HTTP server, and the two read routes the launch form needs (/status and /quote), plus the typed web/worker contract, the pure minimum and estimate math, and the shared launch rules module.

Purpose: web must never touch the chain (D-15, FEE-10), so every live value (fees, minimum, pause state, quote) comes from the worker. The contracts (api.ts, rules.ts) are defined first so later plans build against them (interface-first); rules.ts is the trust-boundary validator reused by the launch form (02-07), the pin route (02-10) and the worker prepare (02-11).
Output: a runnable worker with /healthz, /status and /quote; api.ts and rules.ts contracts; math additions; all with tests.
</objective>
