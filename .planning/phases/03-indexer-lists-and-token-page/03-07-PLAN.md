**As a** trader, **I want to** see live lists and charts of every BONDR coin, **so that** I can find and judge coins.

<objective>
Trades flow in on their own: the worker holds one websocket on the BONDR mint, ingests every matching transaction, fills gaps on (re)connect and on each new coin, and keeps holder counts current under the Helius and Neon budgets.

Purpose: lists, charts and the feed show real activity, not just launches (TOKEN-02 holder count, LIST-04 live numbers at the data layer).
Output: stream.ts, holders.ts, gap fill in ingest.ts, worker boot wiring, and a devnet proof.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Helius websocket -> worker | Notifications are hints; the fetched tx is the source of truth |
| any wallet -> BONDR mint mentions | Anyone can send transactions that trigger fetches |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-07-01 | Spoofing | websocket notifications | mitigate | Notifications only queue a signature; rows come from fetchTx plus the emit_cpi authority check in events.ts |
| T-03-07-02 | Denial of service | spam txs mentioning BONDR | mitigate | Prefilter on err and pump or PumpSwap invoke; de-duplicated serial queue; unknown mints write nothing |
| T-03-07-03 | Denial of service | Helius credits | mitigate | One logsSubscribe; holder recount only after trades and at most every 5 min per coin; ponytail upgrade path noted |
| T-03-07-04 | Information disclosure | websocket URL in logs | mitigate | wsUrl from net.ts; every log line through redact() |
| T-03-07-05 | Tampering | reorg of a confirmed block | accept | Confirmed rollback not expected in practice (A3); idempotent keys keep re-ingest safe |
</threat_model>
