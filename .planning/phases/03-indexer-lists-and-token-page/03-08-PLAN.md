## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** see live lists and charts of every BONDR coin, **so that** I can find and judge coins.

<objective>
The live pipe: the worker publishes on every inserted trade, launch, bond and holder recount, and each web process fans that out to browsers over same-origin SSE with replay. 03-09 puts it on screen.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 (live without reload) within the Neon budget (D-04): open browsers cost no database reads.
Output: worker events route, web hub, the SSE route with limits, and a devnet check that a real trade reaches /api/live.
</objective>
