## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** buy and sell BONDR coins with SOL in one signature, **so that** I never leave the page.

<objective>
The chain half of the quote slice: the worker reads each coin's curve or pool state once per 2 s, builds the quote inputs, and answers exact route quotes and wallet balances behind input checks. 04-03 puts it on the token page.

Purpose: quotes are the one trade capability that needs no wallet (D-10); the cache keeps many viewers at one Helius read per coin per 2 s.
Output: core reader with hopInputs, worker caches and handlers, the two worker routes.
</objective>
