**As a** trader, **I want to** buy and sell BONDR coins with SOL in one signature, **so that** I never leave the page.

Purpose: TRADE-01 and TRADE-02 need one transaction the user signs once; the stored bytes are what submit (04-06) compares against.
Output: shared error map in core, the trade store, prepareTrade with its tests.
</objective>
