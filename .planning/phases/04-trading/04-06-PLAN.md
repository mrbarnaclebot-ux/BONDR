## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** buy and sell BONDR coins with SOL in one signature, **so that** I never leave the page.

<objective>
The send half of a trade in the worker: accept only the exact transaction it built, signed by its wallet, send it once, settle it in the background, serve its state from memory; then route prepare, submit and view and prove a live devnet prepare.

Purpose: the byte compare keeps the worker from becoming a free relay on its Helius key (T3), and the landed amounts give the result line its numbers (D-17).
Output: trade-submit.ts with tests, the settle export, the landed amount helpers in core, three worker routes, the live prepare check. The web routes come in 04-07.
</objective>
