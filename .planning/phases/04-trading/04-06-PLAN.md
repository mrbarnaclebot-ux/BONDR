## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** buy and sell BONDR coins with SOL in one signature, **so that** I never leave the page.

<objective>
The send half of a trade: the worker accepts only the exact transaction it built, signed by the session wallet, sends it once, settles it in the background, and serves its state from memory; the web exposes submit and the poll.

Purpose: the byte compare keeps the worker from becoming a free relay on its Helius key (T3), and the landed amounts give the result line its numbers (D-17).
Output: trade-submit.ts with tests, the settle export, the landed amount helpers in core, the web submit and poll routes.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-06-01 (T3) | Elevation of privilege | submitTrade | mitigate | Raw byte compare with sameMessage(stored, wire, 1) plus signatureOk for slot 0 against the stored wallet; wire capped at 1,232 bytes; stored message passed checkMessage at prepare |
| T-04-06-02 (T5) | Spoofing | trade id | mitigate | 16 random bytes, entry bound to the wallet, 404 for another session, 120 s TTL, state machine; repeat submit is a read |
| T-04-06-03 (T8) | Tampering | settle | mitigate | Never resend; unknown keeps its signature for the Solscan link (send.ts rule) |
| T-04-06-04 (T2) | Spoofing | submit and poll routes | mitigate | Wallet from sessionWallet only; poll without a session is 404 |
| T-04-06-05 (T6) | Denial of service | submit and poll | mitigate | 30 submits per 10 min per IP and wallet, 240 polls per min per IP, poll costs no RPC or database read |
| T-04-06-06 (T9) | Information disclosure | settle errors | mitigate | redact on every logged error; view returns only state, signature, amountOut and code |
</threat_model>
