## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** buy and sell BONDR coins with SOL in one signature, **so that** I never leave the page.

<objective>
Finish the trade slice's error and warning states: every result and failure says what happened in plain words, impact and migration warnings appear before signing, and screen readers hear the quote and the trade steps.

Purpose: D-13 and D-17 are locked copy; a silent or vague failure on a money action is the fastest way to lose a trader's trust.
Output: the full result map, the callout slot, the live region, stubbed e2e for every state.
</objective>
