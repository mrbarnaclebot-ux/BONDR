# Phase 4 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the trade panel, the mobile Buy / Sell bar and trade sheet, and the token page layout change. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> This spec extends `.planning/phases/03-indexer-lists-and-token-page/03-UI-SPEC.md` (approved), which extends `02-UI-SPEC.md`. Every earlier token, type role, color rule, component, motion rule and accessibility rule still applies and is not repeated unless Phase 4 changes how it is used. Layout source of truth: the approved `token.html` trade panel (markup lines 282-312, CSS 100-127 and 168-177, JS 442-500). Locked decisions: `04-CONTEXT.md` D-01 to D-19. The owner delegated every open design choice on 2026-10-24; rows marked "auto-selected (owner-delegated)" take the mockup's choice, or the most conventional option where the mockup is silent.

## Breakpoints and Layout

Verification widths: 390, 768, 1440 (plus 1100 for the switch). No horizontal page scroll from 320px up.

| Width | Trade surface | Page layout |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| below 1100px | Sticky Buy / Sell bar fixed to the bottom; the trade panel opens in a bottom sheet. Sheet width 100% below 768px; `min(560px, 100%)` centered from 768px to 1099px | One column: identity bar, CA row, chart, Bond and burn, Trades, note. Bottom padding 80px + safe area |
| 1100px and up | Trade panel inline, top of the 380px right column. No bar, no sheet | Grid `minmax(0,1fr) 380px`, 32px column gap, 16px row gap. Left column: chart, then Trades. Right column (spans both rows, `align-self: start`): Trade panel, then Bond and burn, 16px apart |

Grid placement at 1100px and up: chart at column 1 row 1, Trades at column 1 row 2, the right column wrapper at column 2 rows 1 to 2. DOM order is chart, right column wrapper, Trades, so below 1100px the one-column order (chart, Bond and burn, Trades) falls out with no `order` rules, and the desktop Tab order is chart, trade panel, Bond and burn, Trades.

Departures from Phase 3 and the mockup (auto-selected, owner-delegated):
- **Chart moves into the left column at 1100px and up.** Phase 3 ran the chart full width with the right column below it. Putting the trade panel beside the chart matches `token.html` (chart | trade) and puts the panel above the fold, which D-05 intends.
- **The right column is not sticky.** Phase 3 made Bond and burn sticky. Trade (about 560px) plus Bond and burn is taller than most viewports, and a sticky column taller than the viewport hides its bottom. It scrolls with the page.
- **Sheet from 768px to 1099px.** The mockup shows the panel inline on tablets (680-1100px). D-05 locks "below the wide breakpoint it is a bottom sheet", which also leaves two layouts instead of three.
- **Note line** changes to "The public fee ledger for {SYMBOL} arrives in a later BONDR update."

## Trade panel (TRADE-01..04)

One `TradePanel` component, top to bottom (16px gap, 24px padding in the column):

1. Side seg: existing `Seg`, full width, `label="Trade side"`, options "Buy" / "Sell". Sell tints the indicator destructive (new optional `tone` prop on `Seg`: `"mint"` default, `"danger"`).
2. Amount field: label row (label left, balance hint right), input with unit suffix, preset chips.
3. Quote list `<dl>`: You receive about, Route, Price impact, Slippage (chips), Minimum received.
4. Fees `<details>` (closed by default): summary "Fees {total} SOL" and "no BONDR platform fee"; body is the fee breakdown.
5. Callout slot: at most one of impact warning, migration pause, quote failure, trade result. Result wins over the others.
6. Submit button: `lg`, full width, label and variant from the state table.
7. Fine print (Caption muted): "One Phantom signature. Quote refreshes every 2 seconds."

Everything from item 1 to item 6 sits in one `<form noValidate>`; submit is the button. During prepare, signing and confirming, the inputs, chips and seg are inside a `<fieldset disabled>`, and the submit button uses `aria-disabled="true"` (not `disabled`) so focus stays on it.

### Amount field

| Part | Buy | Sell |
|------|-----|------|
| Label | "You pay" | "You sell" |
| Balance hint | "Balance {sol} SOL" (4 decimals) | "Balance {tokens} {SYMBOL}" |
| Unit suffix | "SOL" | "{SYMBOL}" |
| Input | `inputmode="decimal"`, `autocomplete="off"`, mono Emphasis, at most 9 decimals | Same, at most 6 decimals |
| Presets (D-14) | "0.1", "0.5", "1", "Max" (accessible names "0.1 SOL", "0.5 SOL", "1 SOL", "Max, balance minus 0.01 SOL") | "25%", "50%", "75%", "100%" of the coin balance |
| Max / 100% | Balance minus 0.01 SOL (covers fees and a first-buy token account rent); below zero fills "0" | Exact raw balance, no rounding |

- Chips are a `<fieldset>` with sr-only legend "Amount presets"; each chip is a button with `aria-pressed` true when the field equals its value. Typing clears the pressed state unless the text matches.
- Max and the sell percentage chips are disabled until a balance is known (wallet connected and consented).
- Switching side clears the amount, the pressed chips and any result.
- Balance hint states: unknown (not connected or no consent) hidden; loading "Balance …"; failed "Balance unavailable" (no insufficient check, presets that need it stay disabled).
- Balances (D-16) load on connect after consent, after each trade result, and on `visibilitychange` to visible. Never on a timer.

### Quote list (D-11, D-12)

Rows are Caption, `dt` muted, `dd` mono right-aligned, 12px inner gap, 8px row gap.

| Row | Value |
|-----|-------|
| You receive about | Buy: "{tokens} {SYMBOL}" (integer, thousands separators). Sell: "{sol} SOL" (4 decimals). Body size, foreground |
| Route | Steps separated by 16px rules: buy "SOL", "BONDR {venue}", "{SYMBOL} {venue}"; sell reversed. Venue words: "curve" or "PumpSwap". Accessible text joins steps with " to " in an sr-only span, rules `aria-hidden`. Wraps if long |
| Price impact | 2 decimals, "<0.01%" below that. Under 5% foreground; 5% to 15% warn; over 15% destructive |
| Slippage | Chips "0.5%", "1%", "3%", "Custom" in a `<fieldset>` with sr-only legend "Slippage". Default 1%, remembered in `localStorage` key `bondr:slippage` (blocked storage falls back to 1%) |
| Minimum received | Quote output times (1 minus slippage), same format as You receive |

Custom slippage: pressing "Custom" shows a row under the quote list: label "Custom slippage" (Caption 600), a 120px input (`inputmode="decimal"`, suffix "%", Caption mono). Valid range 0.1 to 50. Invalid shows `FieldError` "Enter a slippage from 0.1% to 50%." and keeps the last valid value in use. Above 5% shows the Caption warn hint "High slippage. This trade can fill up to {s}% below the quote." The custom value is remembered like the chips.

With no amount, every value cell shows "–" (muted) and the fees summary shows "Fees –".

### Fees breakdown (D-01, D-11, TRADE-04)

All percentages come from the quote (live FeeConfig and Global), never from constants in the web app. Rows are Caption; sub-rows indent 12px.

| Row | Coin on curve | Coin on PumpSwap |
|-----|---------------|------------------|
| Coin hop title | "Fees on the {SYMBOL} curve, {pct}%" | "Fees on {SYMBOL} PumpSwap, {pct}%" |
| Sub | "Pump protocol {p}%" | "LP {lp}%" and "Pump protocol {p}%" (two rows) |
| Sub | "Creator {c}%, to the {SYMBOL} fee wallet" | Same |
| BONDR hop | "SOL to BONDR on the BONDR curve, {pct}%" or "SOL to BONDR on BONDR PumpSwap, {pct}%" (sell: "BONDR to SOL …") | Same |
| Platform | "BONDR platform fee" with value "0" in leaf | Same |
| Network | "Network fee" with value "up to {cap} SOL" (base fee plus the D-08 priority cap), muted, not part of the total | Same |

Values are SOL, 4 decimals, "<0.0001" below that. The summary total is the sum of the coin hop and BONDR hop fees in SOL. Summary marker: "+" closed, "−" (U+2212) open, mono muted, as in the mockup; native `<details>` gives the keyboard and expanded state.

Departure (auto-selected, owner-delegated): the mockup's creator lines say "to GRUB burn budget" and "split 80 deployer / 20 treasury". Those describe the Phase 5 fee engine, which does not run yet, so Phase 4 says where the fee lands today. Phase 5 restores the mockup copy.

### Quote refresh (D-12)

- First quote 400ms after the last keystroke (Phase 2 debounce). Presets, slippage and side changes quote at once.
- While a quote is loading, the previous values stay at 60% opacity with `aria-busy="true"` on the `<dl>`; on the first quote the cells show "–".
- Refresh every 2s while all hold: an amount is set, the panel is visible (sheet open, or the column panel intersecting the viewport), `document.visibilityState === "visible"`, no trade is in flight.
- After 60s with no input in the panel, refresh stops: values dim to 60% and the submit button becomes the paused state. Any input or the button resumes it.
- Refreshed values replace in place with no flash or animation.
- Quotes do not need a wallet (D-10); not-connected users see full quotes.

### Callouts

Existing `Callout` component, in the callout slot above the submit button.

| Condition | Variant | Copy |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Impact over 5%, up to 15% (D-13) | warn | **"High price impact."** "This trade moves the price {pct}%. A smaller amount fills closer to the current price." |
| Impact over 15% (D-13) | error | **"Very high price impact."** "This trade moves the price {pct}%, so you receive far less than the current price suggests. Check the amounts before you continue." |
| Coin complete, not yet on PumpSwap | warn | "Trading pauses for a few seconds while {SYMBOL} moves to PumpSwap." |
| BONDR complete, not yet on PumpSwap | warn | "Trading pauses for a few seconds while BONDR moves to PumpSwap." |
| Quote failed | warn | "Could not get a quote. Trying again." (clears on the next good quote) |

The migration callouts follow the live venue from `coin-live` for the coin and the quote's BONDR venue; they clear on their own when trading resumes.

Checked top to bottom; the first match wins.

| State | Label | Variant | Behaviour |
|-------|-------|---------|-----------|

| Connecting | "Opening Phantom…" | primary, disabled | Back to not connected if the user closes Phantom, no error |

| Coin or BONDR migrating | "Trading paused" | primary, disabled | Migration callout shown |
| No amount, zero, or invalid | "Enter an amount" | primary, disabled | Invalid also shows the field error |

Amount formats in labels: buy SOL as typed with at least 2 decimals ("1.00", "0.125"); sell tokens with thousands separators and at most 6 decimals, trailing zeros trimmed ("1,234", "0.5"). The over-15% check uses the quote shown at press time; prepare re-simulates and fails with the slippage error if the price moved beyond slippage.

### Results (D-17)

After a result the button returns to its action label for the current input (the amount is cleared on success, kept on failure). The result stays until the next input in the panel, a side change, or the sheet closing.

| Outcome | Callout | Copy |
|---------|---------|------|
| Confirmed, buy | success line (see below) | "Bought {tokens} {SYMBOL}." plus `TxLink` "View on Solscan" |
| Confirmed, sell | success line | "Sold for {sol} SOL." plus `TxLink` "View on Solscan" |
| Slippage exceeded (on-chain or at prepare) | error | "Price moved more than your slippage. Try again or raise slippage." |
| Not enough SOL (server) | error | "Not enough SOL for this trade and fees." |
| Migration window, pump 6107 | warn | "Trading pauses for a few seconds while BONDR moves to PumpSwap." ({SYMBOL} when the coin is the one migrating) |
| Signature declined | info | "Signature declined." |
| Expired | error | "Transaction expired. Try again." |
| Prepare failed or worker unreachable | error | "Could not build your trade. Nothing was sent. Try again." |
| Rate limited | error | "Too many trades from this wallet. Wait a minute, then try again." |
| Account switched before submit | error | "Phantom switched accounts. Nothing was sent. Try again." |
| Signed message changed (submit refused) | error | "The signed transaction did not match the one BONDR built, so nothing was sent. Try again." |
| Failed on-chain, other | error | "Your trade failed on-chain. Only the network fee left your wallet. Try again." |
| Sent, confirmation unknown | warn | "Your trade was sent but is not confirmed yet. Check Solscan before you trade again." plus `TxLink` |
| Consent required (server) | none | Goes to the "Accept terms to trade" state and reopens consent |

An unknown code falls back to "Could not build your trade…", so no failure is silent. Amounts in the success line come from the confirmed transaction as returned by the worker; if it returns none, the line reads "Bought {SYMBOL}." / "Sold {SYMBOL}.".

Success line: a `role="status"` row in the callout slot, `Callout` box style `border-line bg-surface`, leaf `Check` icon, Label 600 text, the Solscan link (mint) after it. After a result, balances refetch (D-16); the trade reaches the feed and chart through the Phase 3 stream with no extra UI.

## Mobile sticky bar and trade sheet (below 1100px)

### Sticky bar

- `<div role="region" aria-label="Quick trade">`, fixed to the bottom, z-index 40, grid `1fr auto auto`, 8px gap, padding 12px 16px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.
- Left: caps Caption "Mcap" over the live market cap "{mcap} SOL" (Body mono, Phase 3 SOL format, with the Phase 3 buy and sell flash).
- Buttons (default size, 44px, min-width 88px): "Buy" (`primary`, `aria-label="Buy {SYMBOL}"`) and "Sell" (`quiet`, `aria-label="Sell {SYMBOL}"`), both `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
- Rendered on the server; hidden at 1100px and up with `min-[1100px]:hidden`. The column panel is hidden below 1100px with `max-[1099px]:hidden`.

### Trade sheet

- A native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, `aria-labelledby` the sr-only h2 "Trade {SYMBOL}". The sheet content mounts only while open, on the client.
- Position: fixed to the bottom edge, width as in the layout table, `max-height: calc(100dvh - 24px)`, scrolls inside (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). Padding 8px top, 16px sides, 24px plus safe area at the bottom.
- Top: the handle (40 x 4px, `line-strong`, centered, `aria-hidden`; decorative, there is no swipe-to-close), then the close button (44px, top 8px, right 8px, `X` icon 20px, `aria-label="Close trade panel"`, muted, hover foreground).
- Opening: Buy opens with Buy selected, Sell with Sell. Focus moves to the amount input.
- Focus trap: `showModal()` makes the rest of the page inert, so Tab and Shift+Tab stay in the sheet. No JS trap.
- Closing: the close button, Escape (`cancel` event), or a click on the backdrop. Focus returns to the bar button that opened it. While a trade is preparing, signing or confirming, close is `aria-disabled` and Escape and backdrop clicks are ignored; the result shows in the sheet.
- Page scroll is locked while open: `html:has(dialog[open]) { overflow: hidden; }` in `globals.css`.
- Crossing to 1100px while open closes the sheet; the panel state (side, amount, slippage, result) lives above both placements, so the column panel shows the same state.

## Accessibility Contract

- axe-core reports 0 violations at 390, 768 and 1440 for `/coin/[mint]` with: not connected, ready buy quote, ready sell quote, impact warning, impact over 15%, migration pause, a result, and the sheet open (390 and 768).
- Labels: the amount input has a visible `<label>` ("You pay" / "You sell"), `aria-describedby` the balance hint and any error; chip groups and the seg are fieldsets with names; the route has sr-only "to" text; icon-only close has `aria-label`.
- Live regions:
  - One sr-only `aria-live="polite"` region for the quote. It announces "You receive about {value}. Price impact {pct}%." once per user change (after the debounce), not on the 2s refresh. It also announces "Quote paused." and the in-flight steps "Preparing trade", "Waiting for Phantom", "Confirming".
  - Results: success is `role="status"`; errors use `Callout` error (`role="alert"`), warn and info use `role="status"`.
  - Impact callouts mount once when the threshold is crossed and stay mounted while it holds, so the 2s refresh does not re-announce them.
- Focus: Phase 2 `:focus-visible` 2px leaf outline everywhere. The submit button keeps focus through the whole flow (`aria-disabled`, not `disabled`). Sheet focus rules as above.
- Keyboard: every control is a native button, input, `<details>` or `<dialog>`; Escape closes the sheet; Enter in the amount input submits the form when the button is in an actionable state.
- Color is never the only signal: impact has its number and a callout; side has text; fee "0" has its label.
- Tap targets: chips, seg, bar buttons, close and submit are at least 44px tall in the sheet; chips are 40px in the column.
- Contrast: ink on the destructive seg indicator and danger button, and warn text on `surface`, stay at least 4.5:1 (verify with axe).
