## Phase Goal

**As a** holder, **I want to** see every fee movement with a transaction link, **so that** I can verify none went to the dev.

<objective>
Build the unbonded half of the Bond and burn panel (budget gauge, window countdown, fee flow), the ledger totals block for unbonded coins, and the Supply burned stat, all from the coin row's fee fields.

Purpose: TOKEN-03 and TOKEN-05: a holder can see how close a coin is to forcing its own bond and where every collected fee went, before any fee moves.
Output: format helpers, ReadyBar, BondBudget, the token-bond shell, totals and stats, the window length passed from the server, and the extended seeded spec.
</objective>
