## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-07-01 | Tampering | stale cached reserves trigger | mitigate | Fresh costToComplete read plus a simulation before every forced bond (D-15); the gap fill cannot fire a job before ready (Pitfall 10) |
| T-05-07-02 | Tampering | front-run between simulate and land | mitigate | minOut = simulated out minus prior balance (WR-08): a front-run reverts instead of over-burning; re-simulated on retry |
| T-05-07-03 | Tampering | double bond burn | mitigate | fee_ledger_bond_burn index allows one forced_bond or pool_burn per mint; forcing flag; has() check |
| T-05-07-04 | Elevation of privilege | wrong recipient of the pool buy | mitigate | buyback recipient pinned from poolState (PROJECT.md item 6); only allowlisted programs (signTx) |
| T-05-07-05 | Denial of service | ops below floor at bond time | mitigate | forced bond and fallback burn are urgent and still run (D-20) |
| T-05-07-06 | Spoofing | Sender on devnet or fork | mitigate | sender path only when net is mainnet (unit-checked in 05-04); fork uses plain broadcast |
