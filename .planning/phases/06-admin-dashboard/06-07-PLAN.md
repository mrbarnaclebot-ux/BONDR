## Phase Goal

**As a** platform owner, **I want to** see and fix every coin and job from one dashboard, **so that** nothing needs a manual script.

<objective>
Give every fee job a kill switch the owner can flip without a redeploy, held in worker memory and the settings row, and stamp each job's last run for the health panel.

Purpose: ADMIN-05's engine side. The gate sits once at the top of each job (Pattern 7), never inside land by ledger kind, because the forced bond's urgent collect and finalize's closing collect would otherwise be paused by the sweeps switch.
Output: switches.ts with tests, the launches switch delegated, gates in five jobs with tests, health.ts marks, the boot load and log line.
</objective>
