| T-06-20-02 | Information disclosure | moderation reasons leaking publicly | mitigate | reasons render only in the console; the public launch copy shows no reason (D-30) |
| T-06-20-03 | Repudiation | unrecorded moderation | mitigate | the spec asserts the audit row the worker writes (D-34) |
