Contract this plan creates (06-23 and 06-24 use it):
- `type WBalance = { mint: PublicKey; w: Keypair; lamports: bigint; bondr: bigint; child: bigint; childProgram: PublicKey }`.
- `evacuateBalances(d, coins): Promise<WBalance[]>` (getMultipleAccountsInfo in chunks of 100 over W and its two ATAs; skips empty W).
- `buildBatch(d, batch): Job` (per W: TransferChecked of all BONDR to the treasury ATA, BurnChecked of all child tokens, System transfer of all lamports to the treasury; rows: one evacuate row per W with amount and sol set, one leftover_burn row per W with child; no facts).
- `packBatches(ops, perW): WBalance[][]` (Code Example 5).
- `EVAC_SIM_CONCURRENCY = 8`; `simulateEvacuate(ctx): Promise<AdminSimResult>` simulates batches through a small promise pool capped at EVAC_SIM_CONCURRENCY (no new dependency), with totals and, only when adminEvacuate is "on" and something moves, an intent for action evacuate with mint null.
- `executeEvacuate(ctx, intent, auditId)`: pauseAll, then per batch engine.idle, fresh balances, gated land, pacing, intent progress (moved, failed).
- `pauseAll(): Promise<void>` (one UPDATE: every switch false and evacuated_at now(), then memory).
</interfaces>
