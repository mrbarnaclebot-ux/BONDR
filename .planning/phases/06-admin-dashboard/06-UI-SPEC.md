# Phase 6 — UI Design Contract

**Focal points (owner-delegated, autonomous mode 2026-10-24; source: DESIGN-BRIEF section 5 "what can hurt first"):** first glance is the top row, meaning the ops balance row and the five kill switch states. The action focal point is the stuck-step box's retry button in the selected coin's timeline. EVACUATE is visually the loudest block, but it sits last and stays disarmed by default.

> Visual and interaction contract for the owner-only console at `/admin`: sign-in, wallets and totals, kill switches, health, the W table, the per-coin timeline with recovery actions and the simulation dialog, the transaction inspector, moderation, the audit log and EVACUATE. It also covers the two public copy additions (the `leftover_burn` and `evacuate` ledger rows, and the blocked-deployer launch error). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> This spec extends `05-UI-SPEC.md` (approved), which extends 04, 03 and 02. Every earlier token, type role, color rule, component, motion rule and accessibility rule still applies and is not repeated unless Phase 6 changes how it is used. The layout source of truth is the approved `.planning/mockups/admin.html` (CSS 12-111, markup 118-215, JS 224-288) and its screenshots `admin-1440.png`, `admin-390.png`, `admin-768.png`, `admin-modal-1440.png` and `admin-armed-1440.png`. Locked decisions are `06-CONTEXT.md` D-01 to D-39. Rows marked "owner-delegated" record a choice made under autonomous mode (2026-10-24). Each takes the mockup's choice, or the most conventional option where the mockup is silent or conflicts with a locked decision.

## Data freshness (D-21, D-22, D-23; hard rule)

| Data | Loads | Never |
|------|-------|-------|
| Postgres panels: W table, totals, timeline, moderation lists, audit log first page, vanity pool, reconcile count | On page open (server render), on Refresh, and after an action settles | On a timer |
| Chain balances: treasury, buyback, ops | With the page data and on Refresh (one `getMultipleAccounts` in the worker) | On a timer |
| Selected coin's live W balance | When a coin is selected, and inside each simulation | On a timer |
| Worker memory: heartbeat, leader lock row, last runs, RPC stats, stuck count, kill switch states, fee engine flag, ops floor flag, Sentry (cached 60s by the worker) | `GET /api/admin/health` every 10s while `document.visibilityState === "visible"` | While the tab is hidden |
| Audit older pages | On "Load 50 older entries" | Automatically |

Polling rules:

- Each poll is scheduled with `setTimeout` 10s after the previous response, so polls never overlap.
- On `visibilitychange` to hidden, the pending timeout is cleared. On visible, the console polls at once and then resumes the 10s cadence.
- A poll never touches Postgres, so Neon can suspend with the console open.
- A poll that returns 401 ends the session in the UI (the sign-in card in its expired state).
- While a kill switch flip is in flight, the poll's value for that switch is ignored.

**Refresh control** (top bar): a quiet default `Button` with a `RefreshCw` icon and the text "Refresh". Beside it, Caption muted: "Loaded {HH:MM}" (local time of the last Postgres load). While loading, the button reads "Refreshing…" (disabled, `aria-busy`), and the DB-backed panels dim to 60% opacity with `aria-busy="true"`. If the load fails, the panels keep their previous content and an error line appears under the top bar.

ponytail: one overview request reloads every DB panel together. Per-panel refresh is skipped; add it if one panel's query gets slow.

- h1 "Owner console" (Heading L).

### Kill switches (ADMIN-05, D-18, D-19)

h2 "Kill switches" with the head note "per job". Five rows (grid `1fr auto 48px`, 8px gap, 12px vertical padding, 1px `line` rule): name (Label), state word (Caption 600 caps, `letter-spacing: 0.06em`), and the switch.

| Row | Covers (hint for the planner, not shown) |
|-----|------------------------------------------|
| Launches | Launch prepare and submit |
| Forced bond | Forced bond and the fallback pool burn |
| Sweeps | Window collect, split and sweep |
| Finalize | Finalize and the distribution crank |
| Buyback burn | The daily buyback burn |

- The switch is a native `<button role="switch" aria-checked>` named by the row's name (`aria-labelledby`). Its hit area is the full 48px wide and 44px tall, with the 28px track drawn inside.
- State word: "Running" (leaf) or "Paused" (warn).
- Flip: immediate, with no dialog and no fresh signature (D-13), and audited. While in flight the switch is `aria-disabled` and the state word reads "Saving…" (muted). On success the response's value applies. On failure the switch reverts and the hint line below shows the error (`role="alert"`): "Could not change {job}. Try again."
- Hint under the rows (Caption muted): "Pausing a job never moves funds. Recovery actions still run while a job is paused."
- If the health payload reports `FEE_ENGINE` not `on`, a warn callout above the rows reads "The fee engine is {off|in dry run} for this deploy. Fee switches take effect when it is on." The launches switch is unaffected.
- After EVACUATE (`settings.evacuated_at` set), a warn callout above the rows reads "Evacuated {utc}. Every job stays paused until you turn it back on, one at a time."

Departure (D-20): the mockup's "Finalize auto-paused after 3 failed attempts" line does not ship. A step that fails 3 times shows as stuck on its coin instead.

## Accessibility Contract

- axe-core reports 0 violations at 390, 768 and 1440 for: the sign-in card (not configured, connect, wrong wallet, expired); the console healthy; ops low with a stuck coin selected; the simulation dialog in ready, failed and landed phases (390 and 1440); the inspector with output; moderation with a field error; EVACUATE disarmed and simulated.
- Headings: h1 "Owner console"; one h2 per panel; h3 "Recovery actions for {SYMBOL}"; the dialog's h2 names it.
- Labels: every input has a visible `<label>`; switches are named by their row; the Coin buttons read "{SYMBOL}, open timeline"; scroll regions have `role="region"`, a name and `tabindex="0"` (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`); filter chips sit in a fieldset with a legend.
- Focus: the Phase 2 `:focus-visible` 2px leaf outline everywhere. The dialog uses `showModal()` (inert page, focus trapped natively), opens on Cancel, and returns focus to its opener. Selecting a coin moves focus to the Timeline h2. Not-applicable actions use `aria-disabled` and stay focusable.
- Keyboard: every control is a native button, input, link or dialog. Escape closes the dialog except while signing or sending. Enter in the inspector or moderation inputs submits that form. The EVACUATE input never submits on Enter; only the button acts.
- Live regions (polite): the action hint line, the moderation status line, the inspector announcement, the EVACUATE hint, and the dialog's `role="status"` progress. Errors use `role="alert"`. Not live: the session countdown, the health cells (refreshed every 10s, so announcing them would be noise) and the "Updated" time.
- Color is never the only signal: every state is a word (tags, "Running"/"Paused", "passed"/"failed", "Landed"), the timeline steps carry sr-only state words, and changes carry signs.
- Tap targets: below 768px, every button, chip, switch and link-style button is at least 44px tall; from 768px, action buttons are 36px, chips 32px and link-style buttons 24px; `TxLink`s are at least 24 x 24 (WCAG 2.5.8) at all widths.
- Contrast: warn text on `warn-wash` (ops row, held box), destructive text on `danger-wash` (stuck box) and on the EVACUATE stripes, mint Coin buttons on the sticky `surface` column, and muted text in the inspector `pre` stay at least 4.5:1. Verify with axe at all three widths. If the EVACUATE prose fails over the stripes, drop the stripe opacity from 4% to 2%.

## Owner-delegated decisions (autonomous mode 2026-10-24)

Each was picked without asking, per the autonomous-mode instruction, from CONTEXT first, then the mockup, prior UI-SPECs and code.

1. **Breakpoints 768 and 1100** instead of the mockup's 680 and 1180. Source: project breakpoints (Phases 2-5).
2. **Session countdown at minute precision**, warn under 5 minutes with "Sign in again". Source: Phase 5 decision 3, WCAG 2.2.2, D-10.
3. **Health "Updated" shows a clock time**, not a ticking age. Source: same as 2.
4. **W table:** columns reordered (State and Next step beside the sticky Coin), the Coin symbol is the select button, 50 rows then "Show all", stuck-first sort, the first stuck coin auto-selected. Source: D-24 (one query, horizontal scroll), D-38 (phone use).
5. **"Burn buyback now" moves to the Buyback row.** Source: D-16 (platform action, no coin).
6. **"Release hold"** as the inverse of "Hold for review". Source: D-05, D-13, D-30 (unhide and unblock precedent), `Engine.release`.
7. **Not-applicable actions** stay focusable and explain themselves in a live hint line. Source: accessibility hard rule, D-16 "says why".
8. **Simulation dialog opens at once** with a loading phase, focus on Cancel, a bottom sheet below 768px, and the Before column hidden below 768px. Source: consent and trade sheet patterns (Phases 2 and 4), D-38.
9. **Change column without leaf or destructive tint**; signs carry direction. Source: Phase 4 and 5 accent discipline.
10. **Moderation forms gain a required Reason field** and stack at every width. Source: D-29, D-30.
11. **EVACUATE simulates automatically on a phrase match**; the simulate-only deploy state follows D-37. Source: mockup copy, D-35, D-37.
12. **Public kinds:** "Leftover burn" (leaf) and "Emergency" (warn); `leftover_burn` counts in Supply burned and `evacuate` in To treasury. Source: D-17, D-36, Phase 5 accent item 22.
13. **Consent prompt unchanged on /admin**; admin sign-in does not depend on it. Source: D-09 (domain-separated messages), AGENTS.md section 2.
14. **RPC error-rate warn threshold at 2%.** Source: no upstream value; a conventional default, easy to tune in `admin-copy.ts`.
