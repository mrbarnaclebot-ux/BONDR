<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| worker prepare -> dev wallet | The dev signs exactly the stored message, tip included |
| worker -> Helius Sender | Sender can reject or rate limit; the same bytes may be resent |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-09-01 | Tampering | tip moved or added after signing | mitigate | Tip compiled at prepare; submit's byte compare (cosign) refuses any other message |
| T-07-09-02 | Denial of service | oversized tipped launch refused | mitigate | Size guard drops the tip and sends over RPC; build.test.ts |
| T-07-09-03 | Denial of service | Sender rejection leaves a launch hanging until blockhash expiry | mitigate | Explicit Sender error triggers one RPC send of identical bytes; submit.test.ts |
| T-07-09-04 | Tampering | double execution from the fallback | mitigate | Identical bytes share one signature; the chain executes it at most once |
| T-07-09-05 | Information disclosure | undisclosed creator cost | mitigate | Sender tip row in the summary and in the balance check (UI-SPEC surface 3) |
</threat_model>
