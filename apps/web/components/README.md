# apps/web/components

- `chrome.tsx`: Header (brand, DEVNET badge, wallet control) and Footer, server components on every page.
- `wallet.tsx`: WalletProvider and useWallet, Phantom through Wallet Standard, plus local consent state.
- `wallet-button.tsx`: the header wallet control, its Copy address / Disconnect menu and the not-installed popover.
- `consent-dialog.tsx`: the first-connect consent prompt (native dialog, bottom sheet on phones).
- `ui-button.tsx`: Button and buttonVariants, hand-ported shadcn 4.21.2 with the UI-SPEC variants.
- `ui-field.tsx`: Input, Textarea, Label (optional marker) and FieldError, hand-ported shadcn 4.21.2.
- `launch-flow.tsx`: the /launch state machine (steps 1-5) and the step panel with its focus target.
- `launch-stepper.tsx`: the five-node bond-chain stepper.
- `launch-coin.tsx`: step 1 Coin, every field checked with `@bondr/core/rules` and the UI-SPEC copy.
- `launch-drop.tsx`: the step 1 image drop zone.
- `launch-buy.tsx`: step 2 Dev buy and exit, the live-minimum check, presets and the debounced quote hook.
- `launch-review.tsx`: step 3 Review, the fee callout and the connect / paused / sign button.
- `launch-sign.tsx`: step 4 Sign, the five rows, the failure table, and the stored launch id that restores a posted launch after a reload.
- `launch-live.tsx`: step 5 Live, the success art, the proof and the contract address.
- `proof-stamp.tsx`: the "Dev holds 0 launch supply" stamp (step 5 and the token page).
- `ca-row.tsx`: the contract address with Copy and the manual-copy fallback; exports `useCopy`, shared with the copy chip.
- `coin-identity.tsx`: the token page identity bar (avatar bleed, name, symbol, meta row with venue tag, age and links, stats slot).
- `tag.tsx`: Tag, the pill label with mint, leaf, warn, danger (admin stuck states) and plain tones and an optional live dot.
- `token-stats.tsx`: TokenStats, the identity bar's Mcap, 1h, 1h volume and Holders row, live from CoinLiveProvider (Mcap and 1h flash).
- `bond-glyph.tsx`: BondGlyph, the two-node curve progress SVG with a per-instance gradient id.
- `token-bond.tsx`: BondPanel, the Bond and burn shell (a size container): head note per venue, the Curve filled gauge on curve and while complete, BondBudget or BondAfter by venue.
- `bond-budget.tsx`: BondBudget, the on-curve body of Bond and burn: burn budget gauge, window countdown (windowOf, FEE_WINDOW_MS) and the four-step fee flow.
- `bond-after.tsx`: BondAfter, the bonded body of Bond and burn: the bond moment (art, orb, forced or traders), the bond burn gauge and the fee shares gauge (pending, plain Pending review when held, finalized split).
- `ready-bar.tsx`: ReadyBar, the 0-120% burn budget track with the 100% and 105% marks (`lg` token page, `md` and `sm` for the home rail).
- `copy-chip.tsx`: the token page short-mint copy chip.
- `tx-link.tsx`: the Solscan transaction link (cluster param on devnet); `label` makes it icon-only (trade feed).
- `token-ledger.tsx`: FeeLedger, the token page Fee ledger panel: head with the fee wallet link, landed fee rows newest first with Solscan links (copy from `lib/ledger-copy.ts`), Load 20 older entries by keyset, the launch row last; live fee rows on top with one polite announcement per burst; the totals block above the entries.
- `ledger-totals.tsx`: Totals, the Fee ledger's five-cell totals block in its pre-bond and bonded columns.
- `token-trades.tsx`: TradeFeed, the token page trade table with the launch caption and rows, Load more by keyset, live rows on top (batched per frame) and the Live indicator; exports `LoadMore` and `useClock` for the fee ledger.
- `coin-live.tsx`: CoinLiveProvider, the token page's one `useLive` stream shared through context (coin numbers, last trade side for the flash, stream state, a trade and resync subscription, a fee rows and resync subscription for the ledger), and the live VenueTag.
- `launch-receipt.tsx`: the receipt aside, every fee and cost from the live worker status.
- `callout.tsx`: info, warn and error callouts (status or alert role).
- `ui-seg.tsx`: Seg, the segmented control (native group of aria-pressed buttons, CSS-only sliding indicator); chart intervals and sort tabs.
- `token-chart.tsx`: McapChart, the token page market cap chart (interval Seg, OHLC readout, states, live candle); loads lightweight-charts by dynamic import.
- `token-chart-draw.ts`: the lightweight-charts options, series, data, Bonded marker and `liveBar` (tested in `token-chart-draw.test.ts`).
- `launch-link.tsx`: LaunchLink, the header's quiet "Launch a coin" link ("Launch" below 768px, an icon below 360px), `aria-current` on /launch.
- `home-hero.tsx`: HomeHero, the home hero (headline, lede, Launch a coin or Launches paused, Watch a coin bond or See all coins, the D-09 pulse line or from the first forced bond the D-24 line, the rail slot) and its art: the looping video with motes, or the still under reduced motion.
- `home-next.tsx`: NextToBond, the Next to bond rail (D-24): up to 4 curve coins by budget / cost from the server render, the top coin with its sweep countdown and ReadyBar md, rows with ReadyBar sm; re-sorted and pruned on the provider's 60 s tick, hidden when empty.
- `coin-card.tsx`: CoinCard, one coin as a single link (art bleed and avatar, name, symbol, market cap in SOL, 1h change pill, bond glyph); `grid` (age chip, horizontal below 560px) and `trending` (rank and volume chips) variants; `data-field` marks the numbers a trade flashes.
- `home-coins.tsx`: HomeCoins, All coins (list, sort and search held by HomeLiveProvider): the Top / Latest / Oldest Seg and search (?sort= and ?q= by replaceState, a full BONDR mint opens its coin), the card grid with the 60% hold while a tab loads, Show more, and the empty and error states.
- `home-search.tsx`: the search box (300 ms debounce, Escape and X clear), the sr-only match count and the no-match and mint-not-on-BONDR empty states.
- `home-trending.tsx`: HomeTrending, the Trending side-scroller (up to 10 cards by 1h volume, else 24h, else the latest launches, D-13), the mode caption, arrows from 760px and mouse drag.
- `home-live.tsx`: HomeLiveProvider, the home page's one `useLive` stream: the grid list, sort and search, Trending, the Next to bond rail and the hero counts in context; a landed forced bond fee event counts up the D-24 line; trades flash every card of a coin, a launch slides into Latest with the glow, the 60 s tick decays and re-ranks unless the pointer or focus is in a `hold` region, and a resync refetches page 0 and Trending in one GET.
- `trade-panel.tsx`: TradePanel, the token page trade section (one form: side Seg with the Sell tone, TradeAmount, TradeQuote, TradeResult and the `buttonState` submit button, fine print), every value from `useTrade`'s worker quote; `placement="sheet"` drops the frame and uses 44px chips.
- `trade-amount.tsx`: TradeAmount, the trade panel's amount field (label row with the balance hint, unit suffix, D-14 presets with Max and the devnet cap error) and `Chips`, the aria-pressed chip group the slippage row reuses (`wrap` gives auto-width wrapping chips, the W table filters).
- `trade-quote.tsx`: TradeQuote, the quote list (receive, route, impact in its D-13 color, slippage chips with the custom row, minimum) and the Fees details with the masked per-hop rows, every rate from the quote; the creator row says where the coin's creator fee goes for its state.
- `trade-sheet.tsx`: TradeSheet, below 1100px the sticky Quick trade bar (live Mcap with the trade flash, Buy and Sell) and the native dialog bottom sheet hosting TradePanel; close by button, Escape or backdrop (ignored while a trade is in flight), focus back to the opener, closes when the viewport crosses 1100px.
- `trade-result.tsx`: TradeCallouts, the trade panel's callout slot: the success line ("Bought ..." or "Sold for ...", View on Solscan), the sent-but-unknown warning, the D-17 copy per failure code, then the migration, price impact and quote failed callouts.
- `admin-signin.tsx`: AdminSignIn, the /admin sign-in card in every UI-SPEC state (not configured, connect, sign, wrong wallet, rejected, failed, rate limited, session ended, signed out), `useAdminSignIn`, the nonce, signMessage and verify flow, and the top bar's `SignInAgain` and `SignOut`.
- `admin-console.tsx`: AdminConsole, the owner console shell (layout and mounts only, under 150 lines): the top bar, the panel grid in the mockup order with commented slots for later plans, the health poll mounted only while signed in, the simulation dialog mounted while `con.sim` is set (`onTx` on the Timeline and Wallets opens it), and `useAdmin` (wallet, expiry, onUnauthorized) for the panels.
- `admin-panel.tsx`: Panel, the console panel shell (section named by its h2, head note, the Refresh dim with `aria-busy`), and `TONE`, the cell tone to text color map.
- `admin-wallets.tsx`: AdminWallets, treasury, buyback and ops (SOL first, the ops warn row below the floor), "Burn buyback now" under the Buyback row (works with no coin selected; aria-disabled with its reason in a polite hint when the buyback holds no BONDR), the platform totals line, and "Could not read balances. Refresh to try again." when the worker did not answer.
- `admin-health.tsx`: AdminHealth, the eight Health cells: six from the 10 s memory poll (heartbeat with the live dot, single replica, RPC, last runs, stuck steps with the first coin as a select button when `onSelect` is passed, Sentry) and vanity pool and reconcile from the page load, then the full-width Pump contract check cell (contractCopy) with Run check now (POST `/api/admin/contract-check`, the answer shown until the next poll).
- `admin-switches.tsx`: KillSwitches, the five native `role="switch"` rows (Launches, Forced bond, Sweeps, Finalize, Buyback burn), flipped at once with no dialog through `/api/admin/switch`; "Saving…" and aria-disabled in flight (the poll's value for that job is ignored), revert with "Could not change {job}. Try again." on failure, a reload of the overview on success so the audit row shows; the contract mismatch error callout, then warn callouts when the fee engine is not on and after EVACUATE.
- `admin-wtable.tsx`: WTable, the Fee wallets (W) table: every coin of the one overview load with its state tag, next step, burn budget in SOL, vs cost, window left, last collect and W Solscan link; client-side filter chips with counts, stuck first, a sticky Coin column whose symbol button selects the coin (`aria-current`), the first 50 rows then "Show all {n} coins". No chain read per row (D-23).
- `admin-audit.tsx`: AuditLog, every admin action newest first in a focusable sideways-scroll region with a sticky Time column (Time, Actor, Action, Simulation, Result, Signature), the first 50 from the overview and older pages only on "Load 50 older entries" (the existing `LoadMore` with admin copy); remounted on each overview load.
- `admin-timeline.tsx`: AdminTimeline, the selected coin's Timeline panel: h2 "Timeline, {SYMBOL}" (focus target, `focusTitle` on Panel), the W Solscan note, the live W balance line and Token page link, the steps `ol` with sr-only state words and TxLinks, the stuck and held boxes, and the action grid. One POST `/api/admin/coin` per pick and per overview load, never a timer; hold, release and re-check POST `/api/admin/act` at once, write the result to the hint and reload the overview. Optional `onTx(action)` (06-18) renders the transaction buttons.
- `admin-timeline-boxes.tsx`: StuckBox (last error as contract words, attempts, the finalize pre-flight line, the step's retry and Hold for review) and HeldBox (reason, Finalize split first for a held finalize, Release hold).
- `admin-action-grid.tsx`: ActionGrid (h3, the buttons in the mockup order, the polite hint line), `ActionButton` (aria-disabled and data-off when it does not apply; activating it writes the reason to the hint) and `isAct`.
- `admin-topbar.tsx`: TopBar, the console's session line (minute countdown, not a live region; warn under 5 minutes with Sign in again; Sign out), and Refresh ("Loaded {HH:MM}", the one way to reload the Postgres panels), split from admin-console.tsx.
- `admin-sim.tsx`: SimDialog, the simulation dialog: a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (bottom sheet below 768px, 640px card from 768px), focus on Cancel and back to the opener on close, "Simulating…", the result line, the net balance table (Before hidden below 768px), the destination check or the refusal callout, the permanent-action callout, the OQ1 estimated note and the fine print. Escape, backdrop and Cancel are ignored while signing or sending. The run is `use-sim.ts`.
- `admin-sim-foot.tsx`: SimFoot (Cancel, Simulate again, Connect Phantom when no wallet is connected, "Sign with owner wallet and send" / "Waiting for Phantom…" / "Sending…", Done) and SimOutcome (landed with one TxLink per signature, sent but unconfirmed, failed after send).
- `admin-inspect.tsx`: AdminInspect, the transaction inspector (ADMIN-09, D-32): a signature field with Decode (Enter submits; a value that is not 87 or 88 base58 characters shows the field error with no request) POSTing `/api/admin/inspect`, the worker's labelled lines in a `pre` (status, fee payer as "W {SYMBOL}" through the overview rows, `#n program: instruction` with indented args, the mapped error, Open in Solscan), a polite "Decoded." announcement, and the not-found and RPC copy. Mounted above Moderation.
- `admin-moderation.tsx`: AdminModeration, the Hide and Block forms (target, a reason up to 120 characters, "Saving…" in flight) POSTing `/api/admin/moderate` at once with no dialog, each refusal as a FieldError on the field `moderationError` names, the polite status line on success, and the list of hidden coins and blocked deployers newest first with Unhide and Unblock; every change reloads the overview so the audit log shows it.
- `admin-evacuate.tsx`: Evacuate, the break-glass section last in the console (ADMIN-11): the three-line list with the W count, the arm field (phrase chip, mono input with autocorrect off, Enter never submits), the hint line per UI-SPEC state, the simulated totals and progress lines, and one lg button (Simulate again, Simulating…, Connect Phantom, "Sign and evacuate {n} wallets", Evacuating…, "Simulation only on this deploy" when the health poll reports ADMIN_EVACUATE is not on). No dialog: phrase plus signature is the confirmation. The run is `use-evacuate.ts`.
