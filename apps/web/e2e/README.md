# apps/web/e2e

Playwright specs (`*.e2e.ts`, run with `pnpm e2e`) and their helpers. `E2E_BASE_URL` points the run at a deployed app instead of the local worker and web servers.

- `phantom.ts`: the fake Phantom (`injectPhantom` with signTransaction and signMessage, a `reject` option for both and the signed `messages`; `emitChange`), consent helpers (`preConsent` stubs the browser's session read for specs whose launch routes are stubbed; `signIn` gets a real session through the API), test keys (`newTestAccount`, `simDevAccount`, `signWire`) and `rootEnv`, which reads the root .env without loading it into the runner.
- `fixtures.ts`: `seedCoin` and `dropCoin`, a throwaway indexed coin (wallet, used vanity mint, landed launch a year back, coins row, launch rows then buys and sells) named "E2E ..."; shared with `lib/trades.test.ts`.
- `coin.ts`: launch-form helpers (`toStep2`, `toStep3`, `liveStatus`, locators).
- `launch-stubs.ts`: page.route stubs for pin, prepare, submit and the launch read, plus `toSign` (steps 1-3, then Sign in Phantom).
- `launch-form.e2e.ts`: steps 1-3, validation and layout widths.
- `launch-live.e2e.ts`: steps 4 and 5. The stubbed group covers every failure state and the reload restore; the devnet group (`E2E_DEVNET=1`) runs one real minimum launch and checks the 5 s success window.
- `launch-recover.e2e.ts`: step 4 while a submit is still in the worker: a reload restores a prepared row, and a row prepared past its blockhash ends as expired, and a refused resend leaves the outcome to the row.
- `coin.e2e.ts`: the token page. The 404 group covers unknown, malformed, failed-only and prepared-only mints; the found group reads the newest landed devnet launch, plus a throwaway landed row with links (its fake mint has used_at set, so the pool never hands it out; both rows are deleted after). The trades group seeds a 60-trade coin: the feed, Load more to the launch rows, the route's 404 and 400, and the hidden Trader column at 390.
- `coin-chart.e2e.ts`: the token page chart on a seeded 60-trade coin: candles at the default 1m, the interval switch, the footer TradingView credit, and the candles route's 404, 400 and launch-price first candle.
- `live.e2e.ts`: the live token page. The stubbed group answers `/api/live` with a fixed SSE body on a seeded coin: buy and sell rows on top with the up or down flash, bond and holders events, one first-page refetch on resync, Live on the real stream and Reconnecting on a failed one, and no flash under reduced motion. The devnet group (`E2E_DEVNET=1`) buys 0.01 SOL of the newest indexed curve coin (on staging, the first curve coin on its home page) and waits at most 15 s for the row and the new Mcap.
- `og.e2e.ts`: the share cards (TOKEN-06). A throwaway landed row on a fake mint checks og:image, twitter:card and the alt on its page, then fetches the card: a 1200x630 PNG with an immutable cache; a coin whose image CID no gateway has gets the 5 minute cache instead (C-CR-01); an unknown mint gets the site card with a 5 minute cache, and /terms carries the site card. On staging the coin is the first one on the home page, and its og:image must start with the staging URL (SITE_URL).
- `home.e2e.ts`: the home page. The hero group checks / is no redirect (h1, Launch a coin, See all coins, the pulse line, no Next to bond) and the header Launch link on /, /terms and /launch (current); the grid group seeds the coin with the top market cap and newest launch: its card, the Top / Latest / Oldest tabs with ?sort= by replaceState, and the coins route's 400s.
- `home-live.e2e.ts`: the live home page on a stubbed `/api/live` with two seeded coins: a buy or sell updates and flashes the coin's grid and Trending cards (and the volume chip), no flash under reduced motion, a launch slides into Latest with the glow and counts up the hero (not inserted during a search), a resync is one `/api/coins?trending=1` GET, and the 60 s re-rank (page.clock) waits while the pointer is over the grid.
- `wallet.e2e.ts`: connect, disconnect and account switching.
- `consent.e2e.ts`: the consent prompt and its server row: Accept signs once and a reload keeps the session, a rejected signature stores nothing, a bad signature is refused, the launch routes ignore a wallet in the body, and prepare refuses metadata the pin route did not build.
- `pin.e2e.ts`: the pin route's refusals.
- `trade.e2e.ts`: the column trade panel with stubbed quotes (preview, presets, slippage, fee rows, impact, refresh and pause, devnet cap); the devnet group (E2E_DEVNET=1) quotes the newest curve coin, then buys 0.01 SOL and sells 100% through the panel with the sim dev key, each row in the feed within 15 s, at most 0.05 SOL spent.
- `trade-routes.e2e.ts`: the trade routes' guards, unstubbed against the local web and worker: a foreign Origin is 403 on quote, prepare, submit and the poll; no session is consent_required; a wallet in the prepare body is ignored (a 0 SOL session wallet gets insufficient_balance even when the body names the funded sim dev); the 31st submit from one IP in 10 minutes is rate_limited.
- `trade-sheet.e2e.ts`: the Quick trade bar and trade sheet below 1100px with stubbed trade routes: open on the tapped side with focus in the amount, Escape, backdrop and close return focus, the scroll lock, 560px at 768, the inline panel at 1440, crossing 1100px keeps side and amount, the footer clear of the bar, and an in-flight trade ignoring Escape, backdrop and the aria-disabled close. Seeds a curve coin locally, takes the first curve coin from /api/coins?sort=top with E2E_BASE_URL.
- `trade-run.e2e.ts`: the trade panel run with stubbed trade routes: a buy (in-flight labels in order with focus on the button, no wallet in the prepare body, only the signature slot changed, the success line, a balance refetch, the amount cleared), a 100% sell, and the connect, not-enough and anyway button states.
- `trade-results.e2e.ts`: every UI-SPEC Results row (D-17) after a stubbed buy: each failure code's fixed copy and callout role, the code-less fallback, signature declined, consent required back to "Accept terms to trade", an account switch during prepare, and the sent-but-unknown warning with its link.
- `trade-callouts.e2e.ts`: the pre-sign callouts (high impact mounted once across refreshes, very high impact then the anyway press, quote failed until the next good quote, a complete coin's migration pause), the 60 s quote pause and its tap to refresh, and the polite live region.
- `trade-stubs.ts`: the stubbed `/api/trade/*` routes and quote fixtures, plus `panel`, `button` and `openPanel` for the trade specs.
- `terms.e2e.ts`: the terms page.
- `responsive.e2e.ts`: the UI-SPEC matrix, 12 surfaces at 390, 768 and 1440: no horizontal scroll, 0 axe violations, and a full-page screenshot each in `.planning/phases/02-launch-flow/screenshots/` (`-staging` suffix when `E2E_BASE_URL` is set). Steps 4 and 5 use the launch-live stubs. `E2E_COIN_MINT` sets the coin page's mint (staging); otherwise it is the newest landed launch on the dev branch.
- `responsive-lists.e2e.ts`: the Phase 3 matrix, 8 surfaces (home, Latest, search, no match, mint not on BONDR, token page on curve and bonded, the 404) at 390, 768 and 1440 on a per-worker seeded curve and bonded coin, through `shoot.ts`. On staging nothing is seeded: the coins are the first curve and PumpSwap coins on the home page, and coin-bonded skips when staging has none.
- `shoot.ts`: `gate`, one surface at one width: no horizontal scroll, 0 axe violations and a full-page screenshot in `.planning/phases/03-indexer-lists-and-token-page/screenshots/` (`-staging` suffix when `E2E_BASE_URL` is set).
- `paused.e2e.ts`: flips the real kill switch; runs alone after every other spec, its two tests in order: the launch form pauses and clears without a reload, and the home paused surface (banner, disabled Launches paused) goes through `gate` at the three widths.
