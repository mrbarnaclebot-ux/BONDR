# apps/web/lib

Server and client helpers for the web app. Files that import `server-only` must never reach a client component.

- `db.ts`: server-only re-export of `@bondr/core/db` (`db()` and the tables).
- `guard.ts`: POST guards: `sameOrigin`, `json` (capped body), `BASE58_KEY`, and the fixed-window `take`, `clientIp`, `rateLimited`.
- `guard.test.ts`: rate limit windows, client IP choice and the 429 response.
- `worker.ts`: server-only `workerGet` and `workerPost`, the bearer-token proxy to the worker; error bodies are rebuilt from the contract fields only (error, paused, a finite retryAfter, a landed or pending lock).
- `worker.test.ts`: contract errors (410, 422, 409) pass through, anything else becomes worker_unavailable.
- `launches.ts`: server-only `launchView` and `launchByMint`, a launch row's public fields (never the message or the signed wire).
- `launch-run.ts`: the step 4 network sequence (pin, prepare, sign, submit, poll) and `getView`; `Stop` carries a refusal's retryAfter and lock.
- `launch-faults.ts`: the step 4 failure table (`FAULTS`, `Fault`, `NOT_FOUND`, `BUILD_FAILED`), pure copy.
- `ticker-copy.ts`: the ticker cooldown and reserved refusals (`tickerFault`, `tickerFieldError`, `reservedFieldError`, UI-SPEC A1 and A2).
- `pinata.ts`: `metadataJson`, `ipfsUri` and `pinFile` (Pinata v3 uploads). The pin route supplies the JWT.
- `pinata.test.ts`: metadata shape, URI length, upload request and JWT redaction; a live pin with `PINATA_LIVE=1`.
- `format.ts`: display formats `sol`, `pct`, `compact`, `short`, `explorer`, `age`, and the Phase 3 `mcapSol`, `tradeSol`, `signedPct`, `curvePct`, `relTime`, `thousands`, and the Phase 5 `feeSol`, `utcStamp`.
- `trades.ts`: server-only `tradesPage(mint, before?)`, the trade feed paged by (slot, seq) keyset, and `parseCursor`; `candles`, `bondTime` and `resyncPage` (first page plus coin row) are kept 10 s per mint through the exported `kept`.
- `coins.ts`: server-only `coinByMint(mint)`, the indexed coin row with 1h and 24h volume and 1h change computed at read (burns excluded); the query is `coinRow` in `@bondr/core/coin-row`, shared with the worker's live events. `listCoins({ sort, page, q })` (Top, Latest, Oldest; 50 a page through `coinRows`; `q` of 2+ characters matches name or symbol by escaped ILIKE or the mint by prefix) and `heroCounts()` (launched, bonded) are kept 10 s per key; `trending()` returns `{ mode, coins }`: up to 10 coins ordered by 1h volume, then 24h volume, then newest launch, with the mode (`1h`, `24h`, `latest`) read from the first row by `trendMode`, kept 10 s. `parseSort`, `parsePage` and `parseQ` check the route's params.
- `coins.test.ts`: windowed volume, 1h change from the last trade before the hour or the launch price, unknown mint, list orders with ties, paging and hero counts (Neon dev; skips without DATABASE_URL).
- `ledger.ts`: server-only `ledgerPage(mint, before?)`, the Fee ledger's landed public rows (`state = 'landed'` and `PUBLIC_KINDS`, detail copied key by key) paged 20 at a time by a (landed ms, id) keyset, `parseLedgerCursor`, and `firstLedgerPage` (the resync read) kept 10 s per mint and hub head; the token page reads its first ledger page with its trades under one head.
- `ledger.test.ts`: cursor parsing, internal and other-coin rows never returned, pages with no duplicate (Neon dev; skips without DATABASE_URL).
- `ledger-copy.ts`: `COPY`, the tag, tone, description and amount cell of each public ledger kind, `ledgerCopy(row, symbol)`, `known` (drops a live row whose kind this build has no copy for) and `launchCopy` for the launch row; tested in `ledger-copy.test.ts`.
- `trades.test.ts`: cursor parsing, and pages with no overlap and no gap down to the launch rows (Neon dev; skips without DATABASE_URL).
- `format.test.ts`: the display formats.
- `use-trade.ts`: `TradeProvider` and `useTrade()`, the trade panel's shared side, amount (as base units), slippage (localStorage `bondr:slippage`), the 2 s refresh with its 60 s pause, the session wallet's balances and phase, one state for every placement.
- `use-route-quote.ts`: `useRouteQuote`, the debounced `/api/trade/quote` fetch; a refresh tick quotes at once without the loading dim.
- `use-announce.ts`: `useAnnounce`, the trade panel's polite live region text (a user's quote once, "Quote paused.", the in-flight steps).
- `trade-run.ts`: `runTrade`, one trade (prepare, one `signTransaction` raced against signBy, submit, 1 s poll) and its `Stop` codes; `trade-button.ts`: `buttonState`, the pure UI-SPEC submit state table (tested in `trade-button.test.ts`); `use-balance.ts`: the session wallet balances. `use-run.ts`: `useRun`, the panel's in-flight phase and last result (aborted on unmount).
- `use-status.ts`: `useStatus()`, polls `/api/status` every 10 s.
- `live-hub.ts`: server-only `hub`, the process's one upstream to the worker's `/events` NDJSON stream, with a 2,000-event replay ring and `resync` on a gap; `createHub`, `splitLines` and `Ring` are its pure parts.
- `live-hub.test.ts`: line splitting, ring replay bounds, mint filter, client replay or resync, upstream gap resync, a failing subscriber dropped.
- `use-live.ts`: `useLive({ mint?, onTrade, onLaunch, onBond, onHolders, onResync })`, one EventSource on `/api/live`; returns `"live"`, `"reconnecting"` or null before the first open.
- `live-list.ts`: the home lists' pure live rules: `applyTrade`, `applyFees` (a fee event patches only `fees`), `applyBond`, `insertLaunch` (Latest with no search only), `decay` (1h and 24h volume to 0 after an hour or a day without a trade), `rerank` (the server's Top, Latest, Oldest and trending orders), `trendingMode`, `union`, and `addLedger` (fee rows merged by id, newest first); tested in `live-list.test.ts`.
- `flash.ts`: `flashClass(side, kind)`, the buy or sell flash (value) or rise-and-flash (row) class, empty under prefers-reduced-motion.
- `terms.ts`: `TERMS_VERSION`, stored with each consent, and `consentMessage`, the text Phantom signs at consent (browser and server share it).
- `session.ts`: server-only consent proof (CR-01): `issueNonce`, `verifyConsent` (Ed25519 through `@bondr/core/sig` and nonce expiry; the consent route spends each nonce once as a `consent_nonces` row), `sessionCookie`, `sessionWallet` and `consentRequired`, plus `pinProof` and `pinnedHere`, which tie a prepare to metadata the pin route built (worker-core WR-06). Nonce, cookie and pin proof are HMACs with `SESSION_SECRET`.
- `session.test.ts`: nonce expiry, wrong signer, tampered nonce, cookie expiry and edits, pin proof scope.
- `cn.ts`: `cn()`, clsx plus tailwind-merge.
- `admin-data.ts`: server-only `overviewData(actor)`, the owner console's one load (W rows with unconfirmed launches, bond sent and last collect; totals with 30-day volume and treasury SOL; the free vanity count; the newest reconcile diff per account from 7 days; the worker's balances and coin memory, null when it did not answer; the first audit page; the moderation lists, hidden coins and blocked deployers newest first with their reasons; the Solscan net), and `emptyOverview` for a failed page load. Runs from the /admin page and the overview op only, never a timer (D-21).
- `admin-copy-health.ts`: the Wallets and Health cell copy (`heartbeatCopy`, `rpcCopy`, `lastRunsCopy`, `stuckCopy`, `sentryCopy`, `opsCopy`, `treasuryCopy`, `buybackCopy`, `totalsCopy`, `vanityCopy`, `reconcileCopy`, `clock`, `since`); tested in `admin-copy-health.test.ts`.
- `use-health.ts`: `useHealth(onUnauthorized)`, the console's only timer: POST `/api/admin/health` (worker memory, never Postgres) 10 s after each answer while the tab is visible, cleared when hidden, polled at once when shown; a failure keeps the last payload and sets `failedAt`.
- `use-console.ts`: `useConsole(initial, onUnauthorized)`, the console's Postgres-backed state: the overview, `refresh()` (the Refresh click, dims the DB panels) and `reload()` (after an action, quiet), both one POST `/api/admin/overview`; a failure keeps the content and sets `failed`, a 401 ends the session. Also the selected coin and the open dialog (`sim`, `openSim(action, mint)`, `closeSim(sent)`, which reloads once when anything may have been sent).
- `admin-audit-page.ts`: server-only `auditPage(before?)`, 50 admin_audit rows newest first by an (at, id) keyset with one extra to compute `more`, the coin symbol joined, params cut to the keys the copy reads and simulation trimmed to `{ ok, cu }`; `parseAuditCursor` (the ledger cursor rules) and `AUDIT_PAGE`. The first page rides in `overviewData`; the audit op answers older pages.
- `admin-state.ts`: `wState(row, memory, now, windowMs)`, each W table coin's state, tag tone, chip and next step from the coin row and engine memory (Pattern 6 order: stuck, launch unconfirmed, held, bond sent, awaiting migration, bonded, finalized, trigger ready, collecting); `sortRows`, `chipCounts`, `firstStuck`, `nextStepCopy`, `budgetSol`, `windowMsLeft`; tested in `admin-state.test.ts`.
- `admin-copy-audit.ts`: `JOB_LABEL` (the five switch names), `auditActionCopy` (all 18 audit actions), `auditTime` (local "HH:MM:SS" today, "Yesterday HH:MM", else "Mon D HH:MM"), `auditSim` and `auditResult` cell words; tested in `admin-copy-audit.test.ts`.
- `admin-timeline.ts`: the Timeline contract (`Step`, `CoinTimeline`, `TimelineRow`, `TimelineLaunch`) and the pure `timelineSteps(t, row, mem, live, now, windowMs)`: launch first, then ledger and migration steps by time (collects folded into "Collect and split, {n} runs", distributes into one step, a sweep per window, reconcile diffs), then one stuck, held or next step; tested in `admin-timeline.test.ts`.
- `admin-timeline-data.ts`: server-only `coinTimeline(mint)`, the Timeline's one Postgres read (the landed or newest launch row plus every fee_ledger row of the mint, oldest first), split from `admin-timeline.ts` so the client never imports the database. The `coin` admin op calls it with the worker's coin-live read.
- `admin-applies.ts`: `ActionKey`, `GridKey`, `isHeld` and the pure `actionStates({ row, state, live, timeline, balances, now, windowMs })`, the worker's applies rules read client-side (Sweep now greys with the next window's time once the current window has a live sweep) (only greys buttons; the worker re-checks); tested in `admin-applies.test.ts`.
- `admin-copy-actions.ts`: `ACTION_COPY` (label, not-applicable reason, tx flag, permanent callout per action), `DEFAULT_HINT`, `fill`, `sweptCopy`, `actResultCopy` (hold, release and re-check result lines) and `stuckErrorCopy` (engine codes to words, never raw RPC text); tested in `admin-copy-actions.test.ts`.
- `admin-run.ts`: the simulation dialog's calls through the admin forwarder: `simulateAdmin(action, mint)`, `executeAdmin(id, signature)` (only the intent id and the base64 signature) and `pollIntent(id, signal, onView?)` (worker memory every 1.5 s after a user send, each "sending" view to `onView`, unknown after 90 s with no answer or a lost intent, never resent); each answers `{ ok, body } | { ok: false, status, code }` so a 401 ends the session.
- `admin-copy-sim.ts`: the simulation dialog copy: `adminErrorCopy` (every worker code to its UI-SPEC line, unknown codes to the worker_unavailable line), `refusedCopy`, `sentFailedCopy`, `simResultLine` (Result, Compute, Fee, transactions past one, Expires) and `estimatedNote` (OQ1); tested in `admin-copy-sim.test.ts`.
- `use-sim.ts`: `useSim(action, mint, symbol, onUnauthorized)`, the simulation dialog's run: simulate on open, the ready phase ends when the client clock passes the intent's expiry, one Phantom `signMessage` over `intent.message` exactly, execute, then the intent poll; a closed dialog or an ended session aborts and never sends a late signature. Phases: simulating, again, refused, ready, signing, sending, done.
- `admin-copy-evac.ts`: the EVACUATE copy: `EVAC_PHRASE`, `phraseMatches` (exact, no trim or case fold), `EVAC_HINT` (the state table hints), `evacSummary` (the simulated totals as Parts), `evacDone` and `evacProgress` (W counts from the intent view); tested in `admin-copy-evac.test.ts`.
- `use-evacuate.ts`: `useEvacuate(onUnauthorized, onChanged)`, EVACUATE's run: the exact phrase starts `simulateAdmin("evacuate", null)`, editing away aborts and discards it, the intent expires on the client clock, one Phantom `signMessage` over `intent.message` exactly, execute (a 409 not_applicable drops the intent, so the button turns simulate-only), then `pollIntent` with progress and one console reload. Phases: disarmed, simulating, again, nothing, ready, signing, evacuating, done, lost.
- `admin-copy-mod.ts`: the Moderation copy: `moderationError(result, query)` (each refusal to its UI-SPEC line and the field it sits under), `moderationDone(op, label)` (the hide and block status lines) and `coinLabel` (a symbol upper-cased, a mint shortened); tested in `admin-copy-mod.test.ts`.
